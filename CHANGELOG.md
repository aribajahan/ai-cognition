# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — September 23, 2026

- Published a combined corpus of 110 unique source records found through Claude Science and
  Perplexity, with provenance preserved for every source.
- Released **AI and Cognition: A Question Map**, organized around nine questions with 111 source
  appearances, evidence-view filters, study details, direct links, and unanswered questions.
- Reconciled the Claude Science and Perplexity tables into one canonical corpus and reviewed every
  source placement.
- Updated the synthesis, methods, research workflow, field notes, README, and map screenshot to the
  same audited census.
- Added automated corpus and question-map audits with planted regression tests.
- Added MIT licensing for code and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licensing for original
  research data, analysis, and writing.

Future evidence updates will receive a new dated version so changes to the corpus and conclusions
can be traced from this baseline.
